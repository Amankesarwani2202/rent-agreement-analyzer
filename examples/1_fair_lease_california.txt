RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Sarah Miller (Landlord) and Atharv Gupta (Tenant) for the premises at 78 Maple Avenue, Los Angeles, California.

1. The monthly rent is $2,200 payable in advance, due on the 1st day of each month.
2. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,200, which is fully refundable and shall be returned within 21 days of vacating, less lawful deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear.
3. The lease term is 12 months.
4. Either party may terminate this agreement at the end of the term by providing 30 days written notice to the other party.
5. The Landlord shall provide at least 24 hours written notice before entering the premises, except in case of emergency.
6. A late fee of $50 applies if rent is received more than 5 days after the due date, following a grace period.
7. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and maintaining the premises in habitable condition, while the Tenant handles minor upkeep.
8. Any rent increase requires at least 30 days written notice as required by law.
9. Guests of the Tenant may stay at the premises for up to 14 consecutive nights without prior approval.
10. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed mutually by both parties, with costs shared equally.
