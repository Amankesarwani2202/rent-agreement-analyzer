RENT AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is executed at New Delhi between Shri Vinod Malhotra (Lessor) and Mr. Karan Joshi (Lessee) for the residential premises at B-42, Karol Bagh, New Delhi.

1. The monthly rent shall be ₹30,000 payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each month.
2. The Lessee shall deposit an amount equivalent to ten months' rent as security deposit, and the deposit shall in no event be refunded if the Lessee vacates before the lock-in period.
3. A late fee of ₹1,500 per day shall apply to any rent received after the due date.
4. The Lessor reserves the right of entry to the premises at all hours without any intimation to the Lessee.
5. The Lessor may terminate this agreement at any time for any reason with 48 hours notice, and the Lessee must vacate immediately.
6. Upon default, the Lessor may without recourse to court lock the premises and disconnect utilities.
7. The Lessee hereby waives all rights and protections available under any rent control or tenant protection legislation.
8. The Lessee shall be responsible for all repairs including structural and major repairs at the Lessee's own expense.
9. No guest shall stay overnight without the prior written permission of the Lessor.
10. The Lessee shall not approach any government authority with any complaint regarding the premises.
11. All disputes shall be decided by an arbitrator selected solely by the Lessor, and the Lessee shall bear the entire cost of arbitration.
