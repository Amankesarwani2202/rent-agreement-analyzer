LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made at Mumbai, Maharashtra between Mrs. Anita Deshpande (Licensor) and Mr. Rohit Nair (Licensee) for Flat 501, Palm Grove Residency, Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra.

1. The monthly rent shall be ₹45,000 payable in advance on or before the 3rd day of each month.
2. The Licensee shall place a refundable security deposit equal to two months' rent with the Licensor, and the deposit shall be returned within 60 days of handover of vacant possession.
3. A one-time late charge of ₹500 shall apply if rent is received more than 7 days after the due date, following the grace period.
4. The Licensor may inspect the premises upon 48 hours advance intimation to the Licensee at a mutually convenient time.
5. Either party may terminate this agreement by providing two months written notice to the other party.
6. This agreement automatically renews for a further 11 month term unless the Licensee provides 90 days notice of non-renewal.
7. Major repairs including structural elements shall be carried out by the Licensor at her own cost.
8. The Licensee may host visitors, provided long-term occupants beyond 30 days are added to the agreement.
9. The stamp duty and registration charges shall be shared equally by both parties.
