RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This lease is entered into at Austin, Texas between Pinewood Properties LLC (Lessor) and Ms. Emily Carter (Lessee) for Unit 12, 340 Cedar Springs Road, Austin, Texas.

1. The monthly rent is $1,750, due on the 1st day of each month.
2. The Lessee shall pay a security deposit of $1,750, refundable within 30 days of move-out less lawful deductions.
3. Upon any default, the Lessor may require the Lessee to quit and deliver up vacant possession of the premises within forty eight (48) hours at the absolute discretion of the Lessor.
4. The Lessee waives any right to a jury trial and any right to counterclaim in proceedings brought by the Lessor.
5. A charge of $25 per nail hole or wall fixture mark shall be deducted from the deposit at move-out.
6. The Lessor shall give reasonable notice before entry except in emergencies.
7. The Lessor shall maintain the roof and structural elements of the premises at its own cost.
8. Rent may be revised as per market rate at any time during the term.
